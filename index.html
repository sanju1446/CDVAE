// Energy metrics & stability guide

⬡ Formation energy

Energy released or required to form a compound from its pure elemental constituents. Negative values (eV/atom) indicate thermodynamic favorability — the compound prefers to exist over the separate elements.

◈ Energy above hull

Distance from the convex hull of stable phases. 0–25 meV/atom = thermodynamically stable. 25–100 meV = metastable (may be synthesizable). >100 meV = unstable, likely to decompose.

◎ Band gap

Energy gap between valence and conduction bands. 0 eV = metallic conductor. 0–2 eV = semiconductor. >2 eV = insulator or wide-gap material. Influences optical and electronic device applications.

⬡ Real-world existence

A compound is likely synthesizable when: formation energy is negative, E above hull is <100 meV/atom, and the lattice parameters fall within physically reasonable ranges (2.5–25 Å).
